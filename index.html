Location

Find your way to Brocks Den

Brocks Den is set in fabulous countryside within the Welsh Marches. An ideal location for walkers, being 2.5 miles from the Kerry Ridgeway / Offa’s Dyke and only a 1-hour drive to Snowdonia / Eryri National Park.

Or for relaxing and enjoying the nature around, we are 4.5 miles from the beautiful historic town of Montgomery, with its 14th Century Castle, pubs, market and shops including the famous and unique Bunners. And 8 miles from the cosmopolitan town of Bishops Castle with its plethora of great pubs and restaurants.
